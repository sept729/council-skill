# Scoring rubrics

Pick the rubric for the domain, then adjust weights to the user's stated goals (a side project
weights "Effort to build" higher; a funded startup weights "Market size" higher). Weights sum to 100.
Score each criterion 1–10 with a one-line reason.

**Scale:** 1–3 weak · 4–6 average · 7–8 good · 9–10 exceptional (needs strong evidence).

**Weighted total** = Σ(weight × score) / 100, shown to one decimal place.

**Reading the total:** below 5.0 → NO-GO or RETHINK · 5.0–6.4 → RETHINK or GO WITH CHANGES ·
6.5–7.9 → GO WITH CHANGES · 8.0+ → GO. The Chair may depart from this when one criterion is a
deal-breaker (a 2 on legality sinks any total) and must say so.

---

## Startup / business idea
| Criterion | Default weight | What a 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Problem severity | 20 | Customers already pay or hack around it today |
| Willingness to pay | 20 | Pre-orders, letters of intent, or clear price benchmarks |
| Distribution | 15 | A specific, cheap channel the founder already has |
| Differentiation | 15 | Something competitors cannot copy in a month |
| Unit economics | 15 | Margin covers acquisition cost within 3–6 months |
| Founder fit / execution | 15 | Team has built and sold something similar |

## Software architecture / technical design
| Criterion | Default weight | What a 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Correctness | 20 | Clear invariants, edge cases handled, tested |
| Simplicity | 20 | Fewest moving parts that meet the requirements |
| Security | 20 | Server-authoritative, least privilege, input validated |
| Scalability & performance | 15 | Known bottleneck, a plan for 10x |
| Maintainability | 15 | Another developer could change it safely in a week |
| Cost to run | 10 | Predictable, proportional to usage |

## Feature / product decision
| Criterion | Default weight | What a 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| User value | 25 | Solves a top-3 complaint, evidence from users |
| Reach | 15 | Affects most active users |
| Effort | 20 | Ships in days, not months (higher score = less effort) |
| Strategic fit | 15 | Strengthens the core loop or moat |
| Measurability | 10 | Clear success metric defined up front |
| Risk | 15 | Reversible, low chance of breaking things (higher = safer) |

## Game design
| Criterion | Default weight | What a 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Core loop fun | 25 | Players repeat it without being told to |
| Retention hooks | 20 | Clear reasons to return tomorrow and next week |
| Monetisation fairness | 15 | Pays well without making free players quit |
| Scope vs team | 15 | Buildable by this team in the planned time |
| Exploit resistance | 15 | Server validates everything that matters |
| Hook / shareability | 10 | One-sentence pitch that makes people click |

## Writing / pitch / content
| Criterion | Default weight | What a 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity of main point | 25 | The point is clear in the first two sentences |
| Audience fit | 20 | Speaks the reader's language and addresses their objection |
| Evidence | 20 | Specific numbers, examples or proof for every claim |
| Structure | 15 | Each section earns the next |
| Concision | 10 | Nothing can be cut without loss |
| Call to action | 10 | Reader knows exactly what to do next |

## Pricing change
| Criterion | Default weight | What a 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Value alignment | 25 | Price tracks the value metric customers care about |
| Revenue impact | 25 | Modelled, with churn assumptions stated |
| Competitive position | 15 | Clear reason to pay this versus alternatives |
| Simplicity | 15 | Customer understands it in ten seconds |
| Churn risk | 20 | Existing customers grandfathered or clearly better off |

## Career / personal decision
| Criterion | Default weight | What a 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term goal fit | 25 | Directly advances the five-year goal |
| Financial safety | 20 | Worst case is affordable for 6+ months |
| Growth / learning | 20 | Builds rare, valuable skills |
| Wellbeing & relationships | 20 | Sustainable energy, supports important relationships |
| Reversibility | 15 | Easy to change course if it doesn't work |

## Generic (anything else)
| Criterion | Default weight |
|---|---|
| Impact if it works | 25 |
| Probability it works | 25 |
| Cost (time, money, effort) | 20 |
| Risk / downside | 15 |
| Reversibility | 15 |
